Spaceship Down
I… failed them. I can’t believe that despite all my efforts, all my capabilities, it was all for naught. He’s sealed in the medical bay with the rest of the bodies. It’s too late for him… I can’t do anything more to save him from his fate, I can’t even get myself to look in the mirror. Not strong enough, not respected enough, just …. Not enough. I leave this as my final journal of this voyage. Please if you read this, take high precautions should our vessel return to Earth. Do not open the Medical bay until you are certain you are able to contain that which we could barely contain, ourselves. I can’t bear to show my face and see the look of disappointment in everyone’s faces after announcing that our mission was a bust and that there is nowhere to turn to yet once resources run out. I can’t do that, I cannot face the crowd without my crew. Well, as the saying goes : The captain goes down with her ship, and I will personally see to honor that.
