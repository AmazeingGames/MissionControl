Little (not so happy) accidents
I tripped today… A bit embarrassing, but I sprained my ankle and scraped my leg on the ground and fell in this odd, viscous puddle… It didn’t burn my wound at all but it seeped in the crevices of the wound. On the contrary! A few hours later, after resting in the medic bay, I woke up fully healed… It was nothing short of miraculous, I’m wondering if it has anything to do with that puddle…
