Ethena 
It’s been a few weeks now and the crew and I have gotten to know each other more. I share a room with Ethena who’s, loosely put, a chatterbox but a very kind lady altogether. She’s also super chill with me playing my music in the room, I guess she isn’t a person who cares for silence. I throw on a little Jazz, the occasional rock and when we want to go into hardcore focus mode, a little Lofi. She’s pretty easy to get along with albeit a little scatterbrained but it’ll get better with time, she seems just a bit nervous about this whole trip.
