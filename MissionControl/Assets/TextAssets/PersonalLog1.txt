Days like today stress me out and make me homesick. The doctor said that I should listen to music more to bring more tranquility into my daily routine but that would mean I would have to ask around for a CD… I could ask Zen but what would they think about the captain listening to something to calm the nerves… I can’t have them think I’m too stressed or they’ll stress and everything will be a mess and I -... I just need some time to think things through.

 I spoke with the doctor for a bit to see how Zen was doing. She did as much as she could to treat it and let him rest for a few hours. No big deal though, we will do a little bit of specimen collecting. Although, as per the mission briefing, we should only be returning plant specimens, nothing liquid nor a living “thing”. This way, on the way back we can study their sustainability and experiment with recipes for wellbeing or nourishment! I’ll be sure to debrief him on that once he’s up and at’em!

I… failed them. I can’t believe that despite all my efforts, all my capabilities, it was all for naught. He’s sealed in the medical bay with the rest of the bodies. It’s too late for him… I can’t do anything more to save him from his fate, I can’t even get myself to look in the mirror. Not strong enough, not respected enough, just …. Not enough. I leave this as my final journal of this voyage. Please if you read this, take high precautions should our vessel return to Earth. Do not open the Medical bay until you are certain you are able to contain that which we could barely contain, ourselves. I can’t bear to show my face and see the look of disappointment in everyone’s faces that our mission was a bust and that there is nowhere to turn to yet once resources run out. I can’t do that, I cannot face the crowd without my crew. ____The captain goes down with her ship - END JOURNAL
