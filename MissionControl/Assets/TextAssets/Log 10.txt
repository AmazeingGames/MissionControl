Specimens
I grabbed some specimens as per requested by the captain, none so biological nor liquid in nature…. But there is one tiny exception that I just couldn’t leave behind with all its potential? Its fascinating healing properties could put my name on the map! I performed some research on the substance in the lab (I have nicknamed and henceforth will refer to it as Puddy). Puddy shows a lot of promise in the regeneration of cells which probably explains my leg’s quick recovery. I’ll do what it takes to make sure Puddy stays in the right hands. Puddy can save humanity, I just know it…
