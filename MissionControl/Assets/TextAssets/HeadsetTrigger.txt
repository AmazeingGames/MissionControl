Serenity
We’ve done it ! We have finally landed and explored some of Kindynos. It is a very colorful planet, fully flourished with arboreal forms, albeit not entirely tree-like. The colors are a tad bit overwhelming however, but I’m sure our eyes will adjust as we progress! We spent most of today setting up a base close to the ship and coming up with a game plan to explore and map out this area as quickly and efficiently as possible. So far, the oxygen levels are satisfactory and the weather is beautiful. I’ll have to come back another day with my headset to listen to some ambient beach noises while I’m out here, it’ll surely add to the serenity of the location.
